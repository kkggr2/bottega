# Bottega: step by step (from 21 November 2026)

One rule: **pay to bring people in only after you've seen that they come back.** Every step says what to do,
what it costs, and how you'll know it worked. Tick as you go.

---

## Week 1: get ready (this week)

**Your room is the shop window**
- [ ] Fill your room to 10+ pieces, each with a line in your words.
- [ ] Your room's ⋯ → Customize room → Who can come in → **Anyone with the link**.
- [ ] Put `bottegarooms.com/r/cagla` in your Instagram bio.

**Numbers you can see** (Claude builds; free)
- [ ] A small private page showing: sign-ups per day, how many hang 5 pieces on day one, add a friend, come back in week 2.
- [ ] **Invite links per source** (e.g. `bottegarooms.com/?via=katrina`), so you see which creator, club or post brought whom.

**Android** (US$25)
- [ ] Make a Google Play developer account (play.google.com/console). Ask 12 Android friends for their Gmail for the
      closed test (docs/ANDROID_LAUNCH.md). Claude builds the app once Android Studio is installed.

**Follow-ups**
- [ ] 5–7 days after your first messages: one short follow-up to everyone who hasn't answered. Mark replies in
      docs/CREATORS_LIST.md.

---

## Week 2: paid creators (about €1,000–2,000)

- [ ] Choose **5–8 micro-creators** (5k–50k followers, film or books, they post their own taste). Start with those who
      replied; add new ones.
- [ ] Offer: **€100–300 for one reel or carousel** walking through their room. You (with Claude) build the room from
      their list; they add their words. Ask for an honest post, not praise: "say what you actually think".
- [ ] Each gets their own invite link. They must mark it as a paid partnership (#reklam / #ad / "paid partnership"
      label): that's the law in Türkiye, Germany and the UK.
- [ ] Ready-made ideas to send them: "films that define me, as a room" · "how alike is my taste to Tarantino's?" ·
      "my followers left me wrapped gifts" · "rate my room".

**Worked if:** each creator brings 20+ sign-ups, and a third of those who fill a room come back in week 2.

---

## Week 3: the first Bottega night (about €300–600)

- [ ] Pick a city: Istanbul or Munich. Partner with one café, bookshop or small cinema (Penguen, tu film, ukino,
      The Munich Readery…).
- [ ] Invite the clubs you've written to, your founders and creators' followers. 20–40 people.
- [ ] The evening: everyone brings one film/book that defines them → hangs it in their room on the spot → swaps keys
      with the people at their table → a quiz on the big screen (Claude writes it) → small prize (books, cinema tickets).
- [ ] Film it: 3–4 short clips for Instagram.

**Worked if:** most people leave with 2+ friends inside the app.

---

## Week 4: look and decide

- [ ] Read the numbers together with Claude.
- [ ] Keep what brought people who **came back**; drop what didn't.
- [ ] Ask 3–5 people for a 15-minute call: "what made you come back / not come back?"

**Go to Phase 2 only if** at least ~30% of people who fill a room come back in week 2. If not: fix the reason to return
first (Claude builds the fix), then try again.

---

## Phase 2 (months 2–3, about €4,000–6,000): make noise

- [ ] **"The Room" at a festival:** a real Bottega room (framed posters, a shelf, an armchair, a QR code) at
      !f İstanbul (February), Istanbul Film Festival (April) or Filmfest München (June). Write to the organisers
      6–8 weeks ahead. Claude writes the one-page proposal.
- [ ] **Press:** "an app against the feed" story to culture journalists (Oksijen, Bant, Gazete Duvar's culture desk;
      SZ and Mucbook in Munich). Claude writes the pitch.
- [ ] **University ambassadors:** 5–10 students, €50–100/month each, running their film/book society on Bottega.
- [ ] **One cinema + one bookshop per city** as partners: "after the screening, hang it in your room"; shelf cards in
      the shop. You pay printing and a small event budget.
- [ ] A Bottega night every month in each city.

## Phase 3 (after Phase 2 works): scale

- [ ] Paid ads on Instagram/TikTok using the best creator reels. Start at €500; raise only if new users are cheap
      **and** they come back.
- [ ] A part-time community person to run the founders' group, events and creators.
- [ ] More cities, where the numbers say people come back most.
