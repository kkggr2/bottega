# Club launch: everything to do

The goal for the next 4 weeks: 3–5 clubs using Bottega together, each with its own quiz.
The message to send is in OUTREACH.md (English, German, Turkish).

## Before you send anything (one evening)

- [ ] **Release / update the app.** In App Store Connect, check 1.0 is "Ready for Distribution". Upload build 5 (version 1.1) from Xcode and submit it, adding to the reviewer notes: "Clubs: anyone can start a club and share its link; members get a club chat (same reporting, blocking and word filter) and club quizzes."
- [ ] **Make your own room your best advert.** Organisers will look at it first. Set it to "Anyone with the link" (Customize room → Who can come in), fill it with things you love, write a line on each, and put something on the turntable, in the TV and on the armchair.
- [ ] **Test with two accounts** (yours and the demo account): start a test club, add to its shelf, ask "What's next?", join from the other account and vote, play "The taste test" in both, write in the club chat, send a letter.
- [ ] **Check hello@bottegarooms.com reaches you** (send it a test email). Reports and replies go there.

## Sending

- One club at a time, from your own address or hello@bottegarooms.com. Never one email to many.
- Fill in every [bracket], especially the honest line about them. That line is what gets a reply.
- Keep a simple list: club, date sent, reply, follow-up date.
- No reply after a week: send the one short follow-up in OUTREACH.md. Then leave it.

## When a club says yes

1. **Ask them two things:** what they'd like their club called, and what to put on their shelf / their quiz theme (e.g. their next book or film).
2. **Start their club** in the app: Salon → Clubs → Start a club, with their name and a sign (📚 🎬).
3. **Send Claude** the club's link name and their theme. Claude writes their quiz the same day.
4. **Send them the invite link** (club page → Invite people → Share link). If they meet in person, send the QR code too.
5. **Once the organiser has joined, hand the club over** so they're the host (tell Claude until this is a button in the app).
6. **Suggest a moment:** "Everyone join before the next meeting, put what's next to a vote, and play the quiz at the end."
7. **After their first evening,** look at the club's numbers on its page: joined · came back · active this week.

If a club says no or doesn't reply after the follow-up, nothing to undo: you haven't set anything up.

## Every week

- Monday: check your club pages' numbers and the reports inbox.
- Tell Claude which clubs need a new quiz (next film / book), and any problems people mention.
- Reply to every report within 24 hours (Apple requires it): delete the message or account in Supabase if it breaks the rules.

## What to watch after 4 weeks

- Do club members play more than one quiz?
- Does the club chat still have messages a week later?
- Do about 1 in 4 people still open the app in week 4?
Those three answers decide what to build next.
