## The small accounts search can't find: 10 minutes on your phone

Instagram, Letterboxd and TikTok profiles barely show up in search engines, so pick these yourself:
- **Instagram:** #bookstagramturkiye · #kitapyorumu · #kitaptavsiyesi · #munichbookstagram · #sinemaönerisi. Open the
  accounts with a few hundred to a few thousand followers whose captions are their own words.
- **Letterboxd:** open a film you love (e.g. *Uzak*, *Kış Uykusu*, *Paris, Texas*) → Reviews → Popular this week. Writers
  with a personal style and a link in their bio are your people.
- **Bluesky:** search "letterboxd" or "currently reading", and look for people posting about their own taste.

Write to 2 or 3 a day. Each message with one specific line about them.
