# A club's first evening on Bottega

What to do when a club says yes, so their first evening goes well.
Everything here takes about 30 minutes, spread over the week before they meet.

---

## As soon as they say yes (same day)

1. **Start the club**, or let them start it at bottegarooms.com/start.
   - The club opens straight into "Add to the shelf". Add **3–5** books or films they've already done together (ask them, or look at their Instagram / Meetup).
   - Never send a link to an empty club.
2. **Ask for two things**, in one short message:
   - Their next book or film (for their quiz).
   - The organiser's email or Bottega username (to make them host).
3. **Ask Claude for their quiz** with the theme. It's usually ready the same day.
4. **Once the organiser has joined:** at the bottom of the club page, tap **Hand the club to someone else**, pick them, then tap **Make them the host**. You stay in as a member, so you can help.

## The week before they meet

5. **Put up a "What's next?" vote** with 2–4 options for the following month. A vote gives members a reason to open the club before the evening.
6. **The organiser sends the message below** to their group (WhatsApp, email, Meetup), with the link.

## On the evening

7. **Show the poster.** On the club page: **Invite people → Poster to print**. Print it, or show it on the screen before a screening. Members scan it with their phone camera and are in within seconds, with no email needed.
8. **Play the quiz together** at the end (7 questions, about 2 minutes). The high score table is the fun part, so read the top three out loud.
9. **Close the vote** with everyone there. The winner goes onto the shelf automatically.

## The day after

10. **Look at the club's numbers** on its page: joined · came back · active this week.
11. **Send a thank-you** to the organiser, and ask: "What would make it better for you?" That answer is gold.

---

## The message the organiser sends to members

### English
> Hi all! Our club now has a home on Bottega: a shelf of everything we've read/watched together, a vote on what's next, our own chat and a quiz on our next book.
>
> Join here (no email needed, takes a few seconds): **[club link]**
>
> Vote on next month's pick before we meet, and we'll play the quiz at the end of the evening. See you there!

### Deutsch
> Hallo zusammen! Unser Club hat jetzt ein Zuhause auf Bottega: ein Regal mit allem, was wir zusammen gelesen/gesehen haben, eine Abstimmung über das Nächste, unseren eigenen Chat und ein Quiz zu unserem nächsten Buch.
>
> Hier beitreten (ohne E-Mail, dauert ein paar Sekunden): **[Club-Link]**
>
> Stimmt bis zum Treffen über den nächsten Monat ab, und am Ende des Abends spielen wir das Quiz. Bis bald!

### Türkçe
> Herkese merhaba! Kulübümüzün artık Bottega'da bir evi var: birlikte okuduğumuz/izlediğimiz her şeyin rafı, sıradakini seçme oylaması, kendi sohbetimiz ve bir sonraki kitabımız üzerine bir quiz.
>
> Buradan katılın (e-posta gerekmez, birkaç saniye sürer): **[kulüp linki]**
>
> Buluşmadan önce gelecek ayın seçimine oy verin, akşamın sonunda da quiz'i birlikte oynayalım. Görüşmek üzere!

## What to ask Claude for a club's quiz

Copy this, fill it in, and send:

> Quiz for **[club name]** (bottegarooms.com/join/[link name]).
> Theme: **[their next book / film / director / author]**.
> Language: **[English / Deutsch / Türkçe]**.
> Anything they love or joke about: **[optional]**.

## If something goes wrong

- **Someone can't scan the QR code:** read out the short link under it.
- **Someone wants to write in the chat:** guests read along until they add their email (tap "Add my email" on the club page). It takes a minute.
- **The organiser wants to stop:** they can delete the club at the bottom of its page, and everything goes with it.
