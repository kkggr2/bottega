# Follow-up to Juliana and the Cita Press team

Thank you for the like! I went ahead and made a draft of your room, so you can see it before deciding anything:
bottegarooms.com/famous/citapress

- Ten of your books on the shelf, in your own covers, each crediting its designer, translator or foreword writer,
  and each linking to read it free on citapress.org.
- Amy Lowell's "Patterns" hung on the wall, from your edition of Men, Women and Ghosts.
- Ethel Smyth's The March of the Women on the turntable (it plays when you drop the needle), Dolores open on the
  armchair, and The Yellow Wallpaper, 10 Days in a Madhouse and Grand Isle in the TV.
- The whole room also reads in Spanish: there's an English / Español switch at the top.

The lines under each book are placeholders, so you're welcome to write your own, swap anything, or say no.
It isn't listed anywhere until you say yes.

Two small gifts, yours to use either way:
- A 17-second video of your room for Instagram (attached).
- A "Whose first line is it?" quiz: seven openings from your books, for your newsletter. If you start a Cita reading
  club on Bottega, it plays there too, with a score table, and readers can read a book together with chapter notes
  that never spoil what's ahead.

Warmly,
Çağla
