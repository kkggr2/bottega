# BookTok / FilmTok: the one message

One message for everyone, with **two blanks you fill by hand**. The blanks are what make it work: creators get
hundreds of "collab?" DMs and skip anything that could have been sent to anyone.

**Before each one (2 minutes):** watch one of their recent videos. Fill in:
- **[THEIR VIDEO]**: one real detail, e.g. "your 'films that define me' video", "the Persuasion rant".
- **[THREE THINGS]**: three books/films they clearly love, from their videos.

**Where to send:** Instagram DM first (TikTok DMs often only work if they follow you back). If their bio has an email,
send it there too, with the subject line below. One message, one follow-up, then stop.

---

## English

**Subject (email):** I'd love to hang your favourites on a wall

> Hi [NAME]!
>
> I'm Çağla. I loved [THEIR VIDEO], and it made me think you'd love what I've been making.
>
> It's called Bottega: instead of a list, your favourite books and films hang in a room. Covers in gold frames,
> books on a shelf, a record on the turntable, and a few words on why each one matters to you. Friends can step in,
> leave you a note, or leave a book wrapped like a gift.
>
> I'd love to see yours, I already imagine [THREE THINGS] on the wall. It takes about five minutes, it's free,
> no ads: bottegarooms.com
> (on iPhone it's also an app: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6832240962)
>
> If you'd rather, send me your favourites and I'll hang the room for you, so you only have to open it.
> And if you ever show it to your followers, they can step in and see how alike their taste is to yours.
>
> Either way, thank you for the videos!
> Çağla

**Follow-up (5–7 days later, only if no answer, in the same thread):**

> Hi [NAME], just floating this back up in case it got buried. Here's a room so you can see what I mean:
> bottegarooms.com/famous/kubrick. Happy to hang yours for you whenever you like 🙂

## Small rules that matter

- **Don't mention money in the first message.** If they ask, or say they only do paid work, use docs/CREATOR_OFFER.md.
- **Don't ask them to post.** "If you ever show it" is enough. Asking for a post makes it an ad; offering a room makes it a gift.
- **When someone says yes to "hang it for me":** make their room the same day and send the link. Speed is the
  whole impression.
- **10–15 a day at most** on Instagram, spread out, or the account can get limited. Email has no such limit.
- **Track it** in CREATORS_LIST.md (☐ ✉️ 💬 ✅ ✖️) and give each yes their own invite link, so /numbers shows who
  brought whom.
- Once @zesframes's story is out and she's happy with it, you can add one line: "@zesframes shared hers this week."
  Real proof from someone like them beats anything else you can write.
